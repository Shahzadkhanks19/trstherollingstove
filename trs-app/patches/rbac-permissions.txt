Add to `PERMISSION_KEYS` in `types/auth.ts`:

"cms.read",
"cms.manage",

Add to `PERMISSION_DEFINITIONS`:

"cms.read": {
  name: "View CMS content",
  module: "cms",
  description: "View banners, gallery items and testimonials.",
},

"cms.manage": {
  name: "Manage CMS content",
  module: "cms",
  description: "Create, update, publish and delete website CMS content.",
},

Recommended role assignments:

manager:
"cms.read",
"cms.manage",

cashier:
"cms.read",

Super admin and admin receive them automatically through `allPermissions`.
